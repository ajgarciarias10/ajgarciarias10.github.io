03ABOUT ME

Engineering Philosophy

~/about/background
Data to AI transition

I am a Computer Engineering student moving decisively into AI engineering — building intelligent agents, LLM-powered systems, and production web applications. Previously working in Data Engineering, I bring strong foundations in database design, data pipelines, and system reliability to modern generative AI applications.

My work is guided by three engineering principles:

Human First AI

AI should empower and clarify human judgment, not replace it with an opaque black box. Algorithms must remain explainable and aligned with the user.

Local-First & Data Sovereignty

Whenever viable, compute should happen close to the user without unnecessary cloud rents, recurring API fees, or privacy compromises.

Production Craftsmanship

From database schemas and network concurrency down to typography and micro-interactions, software is incomplete until it is resilient, performant, and delightful to use.
